What is not included

Better said now than discovered on the invoice.

Limits of these packages

  • No online shop. Payments, stock and delivery are a different build. Ask for a quote if you sell products.
  • No copywriting by default. You supply the text, or it is added to the scope and price.
  • No logo or brand design. An existing logo is used; a new one is separate work.
  • No visual editing. The site is static HTML, so text changes are made in code unless a content management setup is in the quote.
  • No ranking guarantee. On-page work and structured data are included; a position in Google is not something anyone can promise.
  • No maintenance retainer. The site does not need one to keep running. Changes are quoted as they come up.

Questions

The six that come up before every build.

Website package questions

What is included in the $249 / ₹23,400 Starter site?

One page with hero, about, services, gallery and contact sections, a contact form that emails you, a mobile layout tested at 360px wide, basic on-page SEO, and the domain connected with HTTPS. It is a complete one-page site, not a template filled with your logo.

What is the difference between Professional and Premium?

Professional adds four more pages and the search setup: page titles and descriptions written per page, canonical URLs, Schema.org structured data, a sitemap and Google Analytics. Premium adds a blog on top of that, with an index page and article templates that carry an author and a date, so new guides do not need a developer.

Do you use a template or a page builder?

No. Each site is written in HTML and CSS for that business, which is why the pages are small and fast and why nothing breaks when a plugin updates. The trade-off is that text changes are made in the code rather than in a visual editor, unless a content management setup is added to the quote.

Who writes the text and supplies the photos?

You supply both by default, because only you know the business and your own photos beat stock every time. Copywriting and photo sourcing can be added to the brief, and then they are priced into the quote instead of appearing as extra work later.

How long does a website take to build?

One page takes 1 to 2 weeks from approved quote to launch. Five pages take 3 to 4 weeks. That assumes the copy and photos are ready; when they arrive late, the launch date moves by the same amount.

What happens if I need more than two rounds of changes?

Two rounds of changes per page are included. Further rounds are quoted before any work starts, so you always know the cost of a change before agreeing to it.

Terms

The same rules apply to every line above.

In every price, and never in the price

IncludedAlways

  • A written quote with the scope, price and date before work starts
  • Two rounds of changes per page
  • One point of contact: the person building it
  • Testing before launch, not after
  • Your accounts, your files, your domain

Not includedEver

  • Domain registration and hosting renewal
  • Third-party subscriptions and paid tools
  • Copywriting, unless added to the scope
  • Photography and stock licences
  • Ad spend, printing or signage

Payment is 50% to start and 50% on launch on every one-off price. Extra revision rounds are quoted before they start. A project that needs something not in this table gets a quote written with the same detail, rather than a surprise line on an invoice.

Process

From first message to live, whichever line you buy.

How the work runs

  1. 01

    Brief

    The business, the trade, the action visitors should take and any site you like. Fifteen minutes of typing is enough.

  2. 02

    Quote

    The scope, exclusions, price, payment dates and lead time in writing: 1 to 2 weeks for one page, 3 to 4 for five. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Content

    You send text, photos or documentation, or point me at what exists. Missing content is the most common reason a project slips.

  4. 04

    Build and review

    Work happens in the agreed order and you review each page or flow before the next stage begins.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Domain connected, HTTPS confirmed, forms tested from an outside email, sitemap submitted and analytics installed where the price includes it.

Questions

Including the ones usually avoided in a proposal.

Questions about prices and payment

Is the price a one-off payment?

Yes. The package price is paid once for the build and is fixed in the written quote. Domain registration and hosting are separate yearly costs paid to those providers and stay in your name.

When do I pay?

50% of the price to start and 50% on launch. The rupee amount, the payment dates and the delivery date are written into the quote before any work starts.

What if my project does not fit a package?

Send the brief anyway. The packages cover most local business sites, and anything outside them gets a written quote with the same detail: page list, features, price and delivery date.

Who buys the domain and hosting?

You do, in your own accounts. Domain and hosting fees are not included in any package because they are yearly third-party costs. Creating the accounts first means nobody can hold your site hostage later.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

The Starter and Professional builds are static HTML, so text changes are made in the code. If you want to edit content yourself, say so in the brief and a content management setup is scoped into the quote.

Do you write the website copy?

You supply the text by default, because only you know the business. If you need copy written or edited, add it to the brief so it is included in the scope and the price rather than turning into extra work later.

How long does a project take?

A one-page site takes 1 to 2 weeks from approved quote to launch. A five-page site takes 3 to 4 weeks. That assumes the copy and photos are ready; if they arrive late, the launch moves by the same amount.

What happens after launch?

The site is yours and it does not depend on a subscription to keep running. Small text changes are handled as a separate piece of work; ask for the rate in your quote if you expect regular updates.

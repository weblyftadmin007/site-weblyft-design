It replies to the questions already sitting in your inbox, captures the visitor's details, books the appointment and hands over to a human when it should. What it does

A receptionist for the questions your site creates but cannot answer.

Five jobs, done on the page

  1. 01

    Answers from your content

    Prices, opening hours, service area, parking, warranties, what is included. It quotes your pages and links the source, so the visitor can check it.

  2. 02

    Captures the lead

    Name, phone, email and the question, emailed to you as a transcript instead of a half-filled form someone abandoned.

  3. 03

    Books the appointment

    The Booking bot reads your free slots, writes the appointment into the calendar, confirms by email and offers a reschedule link.

  4. 04

    Qualifies the job

    The Quote bot asks the three or four questions you would ask on the phone, then sends a structured brief instead of "please call me".

  5. 05

    Knows when to stop

    If the answer is not in your content, it says so and offers the phone number. A bot that guesses at prices or guarantees costs you more than it saves.

Limits

A bot sold without limits is a bot that will make something up.

What it will not do

  • Answer from anything other than the content you supply.
  • Quote a price you have not published or approved.
  • Replace a phone call in an emergency, or a human in a complaint.
  • Work without a monthly fee, because the model usage is a real cost.
  • Guarantee that a visitor prefers it to a form. Some will not, which is why the form stays.

If a business wants a bot that invents discounts to close a lead, this is the wrong studio. The value is answering accurately at 2am, not improvising.

Questions

The six that come up before a chatbot build.

Chatbot questions

Can the chatbot answer questions the website does not cover?

Not by design. The bot answers from your pages, price list and any documents you supply, and says so when an answer is not there. That is what stops it inventing a price or a promise. Adding a document or a page extends what it can answer.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting the bot and the model usage behind it for normal traffic on a local business site. Sites with heavy chat volume are re-quoted before the month starts, not billed by surprise.

Will it book real appointments?

The Booking bot reads free slots from your Google Calendar, writes the appointment in, emails the customer a confirmation and gives them a link to reschedule. You can block times and set a minimum notice period.

Does it replace the contact form?

No. Keep the form. Some people will always prefer it, and it is the fallback if the bot service has an outage. The bot is an addition for the visitors who would otherwise leave with a question unanswered.

How long does setup take?

One to two weeks, assuming the site content and calendar access are ready. Content training and conversation design happen first, then the widget is embedded and tested with real questions before it goes live.

Where do the conversations go?

Every conversation is emailed to you with the visitor's contact details, and the transcript is kept in your own account. Nothing is used to train a public model, and the bot is removed from the site the day you stop paying for it.

Privacy policy

Short version: this site collects what you type into the contact form or the chat widget, and nothing else. No advertising trackers, no analytics script, no data sold or shared for marketing.

Applies to weblyftgo.online Last updated 18 November 2026

Short answer

Weblyft Design is the web studio of Manoj Kumar, based in New Delhi, India. This site collects the details you enter in the contact form (name, email, business name, what you need, your message) and the messages you type into the chat widget. Those go to the studio so it can reply. Nothing is sold, and no advertising or analytics script runs on this site.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Weblyft Design, operated by Manoj Kumar, New Delhi, India. For anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your data: hello@weblyftgo.online or +91 98164 99417.

This policy covers this website only. Client websites built by Weblyft Design have their own policies, and the client is responsible for those.

3. What this site does not do

  • No advertising pixels, no retargeting, no social media trackers.
  • No analytics script is installed. Page views are not counted per visitor.
  • No newsletter and no marketing list. Enquiries are answered and that is where it ends.
  • No selling, renting or trading of personal data, to anyone, for any price.
  • No profile building and no automated decisions that affect you.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and needs no cookie banner. Two things can still leave a trace:

  • Security. Cloudflare, which serves the site, may set a short-lived cookie when it checks that a request is not automated. It holds no profile of you.
  • The chat widget. It stores a small amount of data in your browser so a conversation survives a page change. Nothing is written to your device before you open it.

If analytics or advertising is ever added, a consent banner goes in first and this section changes before the script does.

5. Why the data is held

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the basis is your consent, given when you submit the form or send a chat message, and the studio's need to answer you and deliver work you have asked for. Security logs are kept on the basis of protecting the site from abuse. You can withdraw consent at any time; the effect is that the studio stops holding the data, which may also end an enquiry in progress.

6. Who else sees it

Only the services needed to run the site and answer you, each acting on the studio's instructions:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs the chat service.
  • Google delivers contact form submissions to the studio's email and spreadsheet.
  • A language-model provider, through the chat service, generates the reply to a chat message.
  • Payment and banking providers process invoices, where a project goes ahead.

Some of these process data outside India. They are used because the site and chat cannot run without them. Where the law requires a contract or safeguard for that transfer, one is in place with the provider.

8. Your rights

You can ask, at any time, to:

  • be told what is held about you and get a copy of it;
  • have anything wrong corrected;
  • have it erased, where there is no legal reason to keep it;
  • withdraw consent, which stops further use;
  • name someone to act for you if you cannot act yourself.

Write to hello@weblyftgo.online with the request. The studio aims to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

This site sells web design to businesses and is not directed at children. No data is knowingly collected from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent details, write to the address above and it is deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

The date at the top of this page is the last time it changed. If a change affects what is collected or who sees it, it is made before the new collection starts, and the difference is stated on this page rather than buried in a new version.

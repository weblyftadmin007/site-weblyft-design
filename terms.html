Terms of service

These are the terms that apply when you commission work from Weblyft Design, and the rules for using this website. The written quote for a project sits on top of them and wins where the two differ.

Applies to weblyft-design.pages.dev and to project quotes Last updated 18 November 2026

Short answer

Prices on this site are starting points; the written quote is the price. Payment is 50% to start and 50% on launch. Two rounds of changes per page are included. You own the delivered site once the final invoice is paid. No ranking, traffic or AI-citation outcome is guaranteed, because nobody can guarantee those.

1. Who these terms are with

Weblyft Design is the web design and development studio of Manoj Kumar, New Delhi, India. "You" means the person or business commissioning the work. By sending a brief, approving a quote or using this website, you accept these terms.

2. Quotes and prices

  • Prices shown on this site are starting points for the packages described. They are not a quote.
  • The written quote states the page list, features, exclusions, price, payment dates and delivery date. That quote is the agreement.
  • A quote is open for 30 days from the date it is sent, unless it says otherwise.
  • Anything not listed in the quote is not included. Extra work is quoted before it starts, never invoiced as a surprise.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars and Indian rupees on this site. INR figures use ₹94 = $1 and are rounded to the nearest ₹100. The amount and currency of a project are fixed in the written quote before work begins.

3. Payment

  • 50% to start, 50% on launch. Work begins when the deposit clears.
  • Monthly fees for chatbots, hosting add-ons or AEO/GEO tracking are billed separately and in advance, as set out in the quote.
  • Invoices are due on the date shown on them. A project can be paused for an unpaid invoice, and the delivery date moves accordingly.
  • Taxes, bank charges and payment processing fees are added where the law requires them.

4. What you get, and what changes

  • Two rounds of changes per page are included. A round means one consolidated set of comments, not a stream of individual edits.
  • Further rounds, new pages and changes of direction after approval are quoted before they are done.
  • The scope is the page list in the quote. Adding a page or a feature mid-build changes the price and, usually, the date.

5. Content, and who owns what

  • You supply text, images, logos and any data used on the site, unless the quote says otherwise.
  • You confirm you have the right to use everything you supply. Uploading someone else's photo or text is your responsibility, not the studio's.
  • Copywriting, photography and stock licences are not included unless they appear in the quote.
  • On payment of the final invoice, you own the delivered site files, the content written for you, and the domain and accounts, which are in your name throughout.
  • Until the final invoice is paid, the work remains the studio's, and the site may be taken offline.
  • The studio may show the finished work in its portfolio and on this site, and describe what was built, unless you ask in writing for it not to.

6. Dates

The lead times published on this site and in a quote assume that copy, photos, access and approvals arrive when they are needed. A one-page build takes 1 to 2 weeks and a five-page build 3 to 4 weeks from approved quote to launch. When content arrives late, the launch date moves by the same amount, and the studio will say so rather than absorb it silently.

7. Third-party services

Domain registration, hosting, analytics, email delivery, payment processing and any AI model usage are provided by other companies under their own terms. They are billed to you or paid by you, are not part of a package price, and their outages, price changes or policy changes are outside the studio's control. Where one of them fails, the studio will help fix what it can, but does not carry their liability.

8. Chatbots and monthly services

  • The monthly fee covers hosting and model usage at normal volume for a local business. Volume that needs a bigger allowance is re-quoted before the month starts.
  • Chatbots answer from the content supplied to them. They can be wrong, and they are not a substitute for a person where the answer carries legal, medical or financial weight.
  • Monthly services can be stopped before the next billing date. Setup fees are not refunded once the build has started.
  • If a monthly invoice is unpaid, the service is suspended, and the widget is removed from the site.

9. No guarantee of results

No search ranking, traffic figure, lead volume or AI citation is promised. The studio publishes what it does and how it is measured, not an outcome it cannot control. Statements about AEO and GEO on this site say the same thing: answers vary, and no markup or file switches citations on.

10. Cancellation

  • You can stop a project at any time in writing.
  • Work completed up to that point is payable, and the deposit covers it. If work done exceeds the deposit, the difference is invoiced; if it is less, the unused part is refunded.
  • The studio may stop work for non-payment, abusive conduct, or a request to publish something unlawful, and will say why in writing.

11. Liability

The studio's total liability for any project is limited to the fees paid for that project. It is not liable for lost profit, lost data, lost business or indirect loss. You are responsible for keeping a copy of the delivered files; after handover they are yours. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

12. Using this website

The text, design and code of this site belong to Weblyft Design. You may read it, quote it with a link, and use the advice in it. You may not copy the site wholesale, resell it, or present it as your own work. Automated scraping that degrades the service is not permitted.

13. Which law applies

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and disputes are subject to the courts at New Delhi. Before anything goes that far, write to the address below: most disagreements are a misunderstanding about scope, and are cheaper to fix in an email.

The date at the top of this page is the last change. Terms in a written quote, once approved, stay as they are for that project even if this page changes later.

Process

Four stages, from first message to a live site.

How a project runs

The point of the process is that nothing is built until the scope and the price are in writing. You always know what you are buying and when it arrives.

  1. 01

    Send the brief

    Tell me the business, the one action a visitor should take, and two or three sites you like. A phone call works if writing it down is slower.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope and the price

    You get a written quote listing every page, every feature, the price and the delivery date: 1 to 2 weeks for one page, 3 to 4 weeks for five. Work starts when you approve it, not before.

  3. 03

    Build and review

    Copy and photos come from you, or I work with what you already have. I build the agreed pages and you review each one before the next stage.

  4. 04

    Launch and handover

    The site goes live on your domain, the contact form is tested with a real message, and analytics are installed if your package includes them.

Questions

The four things owners ask before they commit.

Questions before you hire a studio

How much does a website cost?

A one-page site is $249 / ₹23,400, a five-page business site is $549 / ₹51,600, and $899 / ₹84,500 adds a blog. The price is fixed in writing before work starts. Domain and hosting fees are separate and stay in your name.

How long does a website take to build?

A one-page site takes 1 to 2 weeks from approved quote to launch. A five-page site takes 3 to 4 weeks. That assumes the copy and photos are ready; if they arrive late, the launch moves by the same amount.

Can you work with a business outside New Delhi?

Yes. The studio is in New Delhi and projects run remotely by email, phone and shared links, so I can work with you wherever you are. The time difference is the only practical difference.

Do you use templates or website builders?

No. Every site is written as plain HTML and CSS with a small amount of JavaScript, then deployed to a static host. Pages load fast, there is no plugin maintenance, and the files can move to another host at any time.

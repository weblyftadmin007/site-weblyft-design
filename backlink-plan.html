Ground rules

The goal is to earn links because the page or person is genuinely useful, not because a link was bought, traded or automated.

Do

  • Build one genuinely useful linkable asset.
  • Ask clients and partners for real credits.
  • Reclaim unlinked brand mentions.
  • Answer journalist requests you are qualified for.
  • Pitch helpful resources to relevant pages.

Do not

  • Buy links or pay for guest posts that pass ranking credit.
  • Trade “link to me and I’ll link to you.”
  • Use PBNs, comment spam or low-quality directory blasts.
  • Use keyword-rich anchors everywhere.
  • Disavow links unless there is a manual action or obvious toxic campaign.

30 / 60 / 90-day plan

Week 1

Baseline + easy wins

  • Record referring domains in Search Console → Links.
  • Claim Google Business Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn personal + company, GitHub, X, Behance/Dribbble.
  • Add profiles on Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and a Delhi business association if eligible.
  • Set a Google Alert for “Weblyft Design”, “weblyftgo.online”, and “Manoj Kumar Weblyft”.
  • Create a tracker with columns: Site, Contact, Type, Target URL, Anchor, Sent, Status, Live URL.
Week 2

Make one page link-worthy

  • Upgrade the existing AEO/GEO blog page into “AI Visibility Checklist for Local Businesses”.
  • Add a small original mini-study: “What I found auditing 50 local business sites.”
  • Add a plain-HTML or downloadable checklist with 10 concrete checks.
  • Add an embed-friendly graphic and copy-paste attribution line.
  • Internally link to AEO/GEO, services and contact pages.
Weeks 3–4

5 targeted messages/week

  • 2 client or partner credit requests.
  • 1 unlinked-mention reclaim.
  • 1 resource-page suggestion.
  • 1 expert-quote pitch via HARO/Featured, Qwoted free tier, Source of Sources or SourceBottle.
  • Respond within 12 hours and only when genuinely qualified.
Month 2

Authority links

  • Pitch 1 guest contribution to a respected design, SEO or local-business site.
  • Pitch 1 podcast or newsletter appearance with a specific angle.
  • Try broken-link outreach on relevant resource pages.
  • Apply to legitimate local awards or “best of Delhi” lists.
Month 3

Digital PR

  • Publish the mini-study as a standalone page with simple charts.
  • Pitch it to local business publications, SEO/design newsletters and chamber blogs.
  • Reclaim new unlinked mentions.
  • Review the Search Console Links report and repeat what worked.

Target list

  • Local/citation: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Delhi chamber/business association, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART.
  • Professional profiles: LinkedIn personal + company, GitHub, X, Behance, Dribbble.
  • Industry directories: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist.
  • Expert quote platforms: HARO/Featured, Qwoted free tier, Source of Sources, SourceBottle, ProfNet, ResponseSource.
  • Editorial targets: design blogs, SEO newsletters, local business publications, Indie Hackers-style communities.
  • Relationships: past clients, collaborators, tool vendors, hosting/chatbot partners, copywriters, photographers.

Outreach templates

Client or partner link request

Hi [Name] — I really enjoyed working on [project]. If it fits your site, I’d appreciate a credit on your [about/partners] page linking to Weblyft Design. Suggested wording: “Website and chatbot by Weblyft Design.” Totally fine if it isn’t appropriate.

Resource page suggestion

Hi [Name], I saw your [resource page] includes guides on [topic]. I built a free AI visibility checklist for local businesses based on auditing 50 sites. It covers [3 specific points]. Would it be useful to add? Suggested anchor: “AI visibility checklist for local businesses”.

Expert quote pitch

Hi [Name], answering your query on [topic]: [direct 2–3 sentence answer with a specific detail or number]. I’m Manoj Kumar, founder of Weblyft Design, a one-person studio in New Delhi building websites, chatbots, and AI-visibility work for local businesses. More context: [one relevant page]. Happy to elaborate.

Testimonial + credit

Hi [Name], I’ve been using [tool] for [use case] and would be glad to write a short testimonial if useful. If you have a customer/partner page, I’d also appreciate a credit link to Weblyft Design. Here’s draft copy: [3 sentences].

Measurement

Linkable asset citations +1 Search Console + manual review
Spam links acquired 0 Outreach tracker + Search Console

What to avoid

  • Buying or selling links for ranking purposes.
  • Excessive link exchanges or partner pages built only for cross-linking.
  • Automated link-building services.
  • Low-quality directories and bookmark sites.
  • Paid advertorials or guest posts with optimized anchors that pass ranking credit.
  • Sitewide footer/template links.
  • Forum comments with optimized links in signatures.
  • Keyword-rich, hidden or low-quality widget links.
  • Creating low-value pages mainly to attract links.

About the studio

Manoj Kumar builds websites, chatbots and AI visibility for local businesses.

Weblyft Design is a one-person web studio. I take the brief, do the design and build, and handle the launch and any follow-on chatbot or AI visibility work, so there is no account manager between you and the person doing the work. The work is judged by whether it brings enquiries, not by how it looks in a portfolio.

Who you hire

One designer, one developer, one point of contact.

The person who answers the email is the person who builds the site

I started Weblyft Design in May 2026 and have delivered three client sites so far: a roofer, a dentist and a salon. They are businesses with a phone number and a service area rather than a marketing department, where the job is to explain one service clearly and make the next step obvious.

I work from New Delhi and take projects remotely, so the studio is not limited to one city. A project usually starts because a business has an old site, no site, or a site that looks fine but produces nothing. My part is to fix the second and third problems, and to say honestly when the first one is not worth rescuing.

The work is deliberately unglamorous: one service per page, real photos, plain sentences, fast loading, and a contact route that works on a phone with one thumb. The goal is not a portfolio piece; it is a site that brings you work.

Process

The same four stages on every project.

How the studio works

Nothing is built until the scope, the price and the dates are in writing. These are the four stages, in order, with what you get at each one.

  1. 01

    Brief and scope

    You send the trade, the town, the one action a visitor should take, and any copy or photos you already have. I reply within 24 hours with the pages I would build, the pages I would skip, and the package that fits.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    The quote lists every page, every feature, the price, the payment dates and the delivery date: 1 to 2 weeks for one page, 3 to 4 weeks for five. It is open for 30 days, and work starts when you approve it, not before.

  3. 03

    Build and review

    Half the price starts the build. You review each page at phone width and on a desktop, and two rounds of changes per page are included. Further rounds are quoted before any work begins.

  4. 04

    Launch and handover

    The site goes live on your domain with HTTPS, the form is tested with a real message, and structured data is checked before launch. The final half is due on launch, and you own the delivered site.

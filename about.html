My Story

Weblyft Design is a web design agency specializing in affordable, high-quality websites for local businesses. Founded with a mission to make professional web design accessible to every business owner, we've helped contractors, restaurants, freelancers, gyms, and salons establish a stunning online presence. Our team combines modern design, fast performance, and smart technology to deliver sites that look great and drive results — all at transparent, flat-rate pricing with no hidden fees.

Based in Austin, TX, I bring over 3 years of experience in Custom Websites, Landing Pages, and Web Applications. I'm passionate about delivering high-quality work that exceeds expectations, and I take pride in building lasting relationships with my clients.

What I Do

Services I offer to help bring your vision to life

Starter — $499

A single-page website perfect for new businesses or those needing a simple online presence fast. Includes a hero section, about, services, gallery, contact form, and mobile-responsive design. Ideal for freelancers and service providers just getting started.

Professional — $999

A full 5-page business website designed to build trust and showcase your expertise. Includes a homepage, about page, services page, gallery, and contact page with SEO optimization, Google Analytics integration, and Schema.org structured data for higher search rankings.

Premium — $1,499

Everything in Professional plus a built-in blog system to share updates, tips, and news. A powerful marketing tool that helps you connect with customers and improve your search visibility. Includes blog listing and individual post pages — perfect for businesses ready to grow.

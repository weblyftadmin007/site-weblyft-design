Definitions

Two terms, often sold as one thing. They answer different questions.

AEO versus GEO, in plain words

AEO (answer engine optimisation) is making a page quotable: one question per section, the answer in the first two lines, facts with units and dates, and structured data that matches what a visitor can see. GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the wider job of being mentioned and cited when a generative tool composes an answer. It depends on who the business is, what other sites say about it, and whether its pages already rank.

What is known

Separating Google's documentation from the noise sold as AI SEO.

What actually influences an AI answer

Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode need no special markup, file or setting beyond being indexed and eligible to show a snippet. There is no switch to flip, and an llms.txt file has no measured effect on citations. Anyone selling either as a ranking tactic is selling a story.

What does show up consistently in published studies of AI answers: most cited pages already rank in the top 10 for the question, and brand mentions around the web correlate with AI visibility more strongly than backlinks do. That is why this service spends as much time on consistency and mentions as on page markup.

  • Entity clarity: same name, address, phone, category and services everywhere.
  • Quotable passages: a short, self-contained answer under a question heading.
  • Crawlable pages: content in the HTML, retrieval bots allowed in robots.txt.
  • Facts with units, dates and a source, not adjectives.
  • Mentions elsewhere: directories, local press, associations, partner pages.

Sources: Google Search documentation for crawlability, snippets and AI features, plus published studies of AI citations for the observations above. Correlation is not a guarantee.

Limits

The part that separates this from a pitch deck.

What this cannot do

  • Guarantee a place in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI answers.
  • Make a business with no reviews and no mentions look established.
  • Produce a stable number. Answers differ between runs, so a change under about ten percentage points is noise.
  • Work without pages that already rank, or content worth citing.
  • Replace the site itself. AEO is what you do to a good site, not instead of one.

Businesses are regularly sold "AI SEO" with a screenshot and a promise. The audit here starts with a recorded baseline, and the report says what was not found as plainly as what was.

  3. 03

    Each answer is scored for whether the business appears, whether its site is cited, and who appears instead.

  4. 04

    The same panel later, against the same baseline. A change under ten percentage points is treated as noise, not progress.

This is the method described in Google's public guidance and in published studies: define a panel, record dates, expect variance, and judge trends rather than single answers.

Questions

Straight answers, including the one about guarantees.

AEO and GEO questions

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI answers?

No, and nobody can. AI answers are generated per question and vary between runs. What this work does is remove the reasons a tool would skip the business: unclear entities, unquotable pages, blocked crawlers and no mentions anywhere else.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO, answer engine optimisation, is making a page easy to quote correctly: one clear question per section, a direct answer first, facts with units and dates, and structured data. GEO, generative engine optimisation, is the wider job of being mentioned when a generative tool composes an answer, which depends on entity consistency, brand mentions and pages that already rank.

How long does it take to see a change?

Eight to twelve weeks for the first honest read, because indexing, rankings and third-party mentions all move on their own schedule. The audit gives a baseline on day one, so the comparison later is against a recorded starting point rather than a memory.

Is there special markup for AI answers?

Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode need no special markup or file beyond being indexed and eligible to show a snippet. Structured data still helps a machine understand the business, but there is no switch that turns on citations, and an llms.txt file has no measured effect.

Which AI tools are checked?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, using a fixed panel of twenty questions taken from real customer enquiries and run on three separate days. The panel is documented so the same questions can be rerun later.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The audit and the foundation work are one-off payments. Monthly tracking is $79 / ₹7,400 and optional, and the GEO programme includes it at $99 a month / ₹9,300 a month for as long as the business wants the work continued.
